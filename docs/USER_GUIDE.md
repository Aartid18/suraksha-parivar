# User Guide - Suraksha Parivar (English)

Welcome to **Suraksha Parivar**, your family's anti-scam shield against digital fraud in India.

## How to Use Quick Check
1. **Paste or Upload**: Copy any suspicious WhatsApp message, SMS, or screenshot into the Quick Check box.
2. **Review Verdict**: Look at the traffic light card:
   - 🟢 **Green (Safe)**: Message looks genuine.
   - 🟡 **Yellow (Suspicious)**: Exercise caution, verify in official app.
   - 🔴 **Red (Likely Scam)**: Do NOT share OTP, enter UPI PIN, or transfer money.
3. **Listen to Voice**: Tap "Read Aloud" to hear the verdict in your language.
4. **Alert Family**: Tap "Ask My Family" to send a calm warning to your guardian.

## What to do if money/OTP was already shared
1. Open the **"I Already Responded"** tab immediately.
2. Follow the 6-step guided checklist.
3. Call **1930** (National Cyber Crime Helpline).
4. Tap **"Download Evidence Pack (PDF)"** to share transaction details with the police or bank.
