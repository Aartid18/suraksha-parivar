# i18n Translation & Terminology Review - Suraksha Parivar

## Translation Approach
Suraksha Parivar uses native, non-literal Hindi and Marathi strings tailored to Indian family terminology rather than raw word-for-word translation.

## Glossary of Key Terms

| English | Hindi (हिंदी) | Marathi (मराठी) | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Family Circle** | परिवार सर्कल / सुरक्षा परिवार | कुटुंब ग्रुप / सुरक्षा कुटुंब | Shared family safety group |
| **Digital Arrest** | डिजिटल गिरफ्तारी | डिजिटल अटक | Fake CBI/police video call scam |
